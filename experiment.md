## What Would Verify, Bound, or Falsify the Selector Mechanism

In the clean whole-quantum registration limit ($\theta = 1$, where any absorbed photon has $\hbar\omega \ge E_{\text{gap}}$ and is absorbed whole into a single winning site $m^*$, with excess energy $\hbar\omega - E_{\text{gap}}$ transferred into photoelectron kinetic energy / lattice phonons or radiated as a secondary QED Stokes/fluorescence photon $\hbar\omega'$) driven by fast, uniform, share-blind quantum-vacuum phase fluctuations ($\kappa_{\text{surf}} = 0, \kappa_{\text{pull}} = 0$), the neutral Martingale selector reproduces the exact Born rule $P_r = S_r$ by Doob's optional stopping theorem. Experimental discrimination therefore targets **four sharp quantitative tests** (synthesized from the Claude Fable 5.1 & ChatGPT Codex review cycle):
