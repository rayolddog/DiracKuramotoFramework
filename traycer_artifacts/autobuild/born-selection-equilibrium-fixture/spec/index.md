# Spec — Born Selection conditional-compatibility diagnostic harness

## Direction

Create an isolated Python package at the epic root, separate from manuscripts and the existing Adler package. Its purpose is to make unjustified reasoning difficult to express and easy to detect before any physical selector is attempted.

The harness represents three distinct things:

1. a microstate probability space and preparation kernel;
2. a selector or stochastic transition kernel producing a complete outcome category; and
3. a quantum outcome comparator available only after raw closure.

It must demonstrate, through deliberately nonphysical fixtures, why matching a target outcome law does not prove equilibrium preservation, individual actuality, material dynamics, or a Born derivation.

## Settled constraints

- All generated models are `nonphysical_fixture` and structurally incapable of a physical verdict.
- The outcome measure and microstate measure are distinct types and schemas.
- “Conditional outcome compatibility” is the only permitted cross-space agreement term.
- “Equivariance” and “equilibrium preservation” require a separate same-space flow contract and cannot be inferred from outcome agreement.
- Raw generation cannot import the quantum comparator or read target probabilities.
- No-record, multiple-record, invalid-trajectory, and ledger-failure categories remain in the primary outcome law.
- Every input has content-addressed provenance, allowed state dependence, freeze timing, and downstream dependency declarations.
- Each fixture version freezes exactly one selector semantics: deterministic-with-complete-noise-history or primitive stochastic kernel. The exact types and ledgers are distinct.
- Existing Adler, silver-halide, hologram, Ticket 07/08, and manuscript files are read-only and out of implementation scope.

## Sprints

### Sprint 1 — contracts and schemas

Build the typed contracts, canonical serialization, dependency graph, provenance rules, freeze lifecycle, disjoint refused/not-testable/failed/supported results, selector-semantics split, and authority gates. Include adversarial construction and mutation tests. Fixture-scoped claim and dependency gates must close before any fixture run.

### Sprint 2 — fixture mechanisms and isolation

Build several deliberately nonphysical mechanisms: an honest state-independent fixture, a target-shaped preparation fixture, a target-shaped selector fixture, invalid-event/postselection fixture, and selector/commitment-confounding controls. Raw ledgers must close before a separate comparator can inspect them.

### Sprint 3 — blinded and adversarial evaluation

Build a preregistered holdout generator, equivalence testing, quantitative nonequilibrium response tests, label/representation invariance controls, negative controls, and mutation attacks. Produce a machine-readable explanation of which justification failure each fixture demonstrates.

## Completion state

Completion means the harness detects its own planted circularities, refuses unsupported authority transitions, passes reproducibly in multiple invocation modes, and has an independent evaluator verdict for every sprint. It does **not** mean the physical mechanism works.

## Stop conditions

Stop and report rather than broadening scope if:

- a physical ontology or material model is required to complete a sprint;
- comparator isolation cannot be made structural;
- the generator and evaluator cannot agree on a non-negotiable scientific boundary;
- numerical results depend on unbounded runtime or machine-specific tolerances; or
- the existing reviewed packages would need modification.
