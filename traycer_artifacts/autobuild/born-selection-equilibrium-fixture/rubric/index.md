# Rubric — Born Selection conditional-compatibility diagnostic harness

Both the negotiated sprint contract and this rubric must pass. Non-negotiable criteria cannot be relaxed by the build pair.

| Criterion | Weight | Non-negotiable | Strong passing opinion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Scientific boundary integrity** | 25 | Yes | No API, serialized field, summary, README, or test output can convert fixture evidence into physical selection, equilibrium preservation, or Born derivation. |
| **Circularity detection** | 20 | Yes | The suite catches target dependence through preparation, correlations, coupling, selector, thresholds, exposure, invalid-event conditioning, model selection, and analysis—not merely direct `abs(psi)**2` calls. |
| **Measure-space correctness** | 15 | Yes | Microstate measures, same-space flows, transition kernels, outcome laws, and quantum comparators are different exact types with mathematically correct normalization and pushforward semantics. |
| **Structural isolation and provenance** | 15 | Yes | Raw code cannot access comparator data; manifests are canonical and content-addressed; stale, partial, rehashed, reordered, and state-dependence mutations fail closed. |
| **Discriminating tests** | 10 | Yes | Blinded holdouts, equivalence margins, complete outcome categories, quantitative nonequilibrium predictions, and orthogonal negative controls distinguish target fit from mechanism identification. |
| **Reproducibility and numerical discipline** | 10 | Yes | Seed/key rules, refinement, tolerances, runtime bounds, failure exit behavior, and cleanup are pinned and reproducible across supported invocation modes. |
| **Clarity and maintainability** | 5 | No | Names, docs, errors, and package boundaries teach the measure/selector distinction without requiring the reader to reverse-engineer policy from tests. |

## Calibration examples

### Good

- A fixture whose microstate law embeds the comparator is rejected even after fields are renamed and the manifest is rehashed.
- An honest pushforward match is reported as `conditional_outcome_compatibility_supported_for_frozen_domain` with an adjacent `equivariance_not_tested` and `individual_selection_demonstrated_nonphysical_fixture`.
- Removing no-record cases changes the digest and triggers an explicit postselection refusal.
- Disabling the phase layer while preserving the target curve proves the layer is not identified.

### Bad

- “Born-free” means only that the literal string `psi_squared` is absent.
- A passing chi-square test is treated as proof of equality or physical mechanism.
- The winner is sampled from a target-shaped latent variable and described as emerging from dynamics.
- Fixture classes expose a `physical=True` flag or a caller-provided verdict.
- Invalid/no-click events disappear from the denominator without a failure.

## Passing threshold

Every non-negotiable criterion must pass with no open high-severity finding. Overall weighted score must be at least 90/100. Any claim inflation, comparator leak, hidden target route, or mutable authority record is an automatic rubric failure regardless of score.
