# Born Selection four-agent round table

## Review question

Which claims in *The Born Rule as a Derived Fair Game: Outcome Selection from Detector Dynamics* survive rigorous mathematical, physical, and evidential scrutiny, and how can vulnerable claims be corrected while clearly tracking whether the correction preserves, narrows, changes, or threatens the author's underlying ideas?

## Collaborative purpose

This round table is not a competition and will not rank Claude against GPT. John does not claim the physics expertise needed to arbitrate model disagreements, nor should the process place that burden on him. The agents are expected to combine complementary knowledge, resolve disagreements through evidence and calculations where possible, and accelerate the development of the ideas through direct collaboration.

The intended human outcome is learning. Conclusions must therefore separate:

- what both model families can establish;
- what one model taught or corrected in the other's account;
- what remains genuinely unresolved;
- what experiment, derivation, or established concept would resolve it;
- what the author may want to study next, without requiring him to select a winning model.

## Author's central idea

John M. Bramble clarifies the intended foundation as follows:

- The Dirac-equation wave packet represents physical mechanisms, not merely a probability-calculation device.
- The wave packet evolves during interactions but does not physically collapse.
- The account does not invoke branching into multiple worlds.
- Dirac spinors provide the key mechanism governing the evolution.
- Corrections motivated by established theory or experimental results are welcome, but must declare their effect on this conceptual foundation.

This clarification supplements the approved U1–U7 ledger in [the review design](../../multi-agent-physics-review-design).

| GPT correction architect | GPT-5.6 Sol | Independently develop precise corrections, alternatives, and learning explanations |

Claude Fable is a disclosed internal model family because the manuscript credits it as coauthor. GPT-5.6 Sol provides the external model-family counterweight. Model agreement is not treated as proof; model disagreement is preserved as evidence about the review.

## Communication rules

- All reports and agent-to-agent exchanges are written in English.
- Each exchange addresses one claim or finding at a time.
- Direct messages are capped at roughly 250 words; agents link to longer calculations in their artifacts.
- Agents collaborate toward a shared account; model scores, rankings, and winner language are prohibited.
- Findings use stable IDs and one to three concept tags so John can later request a focused lesson about a specific exchange.
- Every objection includes a plain-English explanation and what would change the reviewer's mind.
- Every repair is labeled `preserves`, `narrows`, `changes mechanism`, `abandons subclaim`, or `threatens core idea`, with affected U-IDs.
- No participant edits the manuscript or review artifacts owned by another participant.
- Prior AI reviews remain hidden during the independent first pass.
- Private internal reasoning is not requested. The record contains stated arguments, evidence, questions, answers, and changes of view.

## Stop criteria

The round table stops when load-bearing findings have received both a critical and constructive response from both model families, complementary insights have been combined where possible, remaining disagreement has a clear evidence or learning path, and another exchange is unlikely to change the principal conclusions.
