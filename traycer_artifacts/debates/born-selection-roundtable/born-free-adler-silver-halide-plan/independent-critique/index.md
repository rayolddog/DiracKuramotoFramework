# Original review question

Could the former carrier-envelope, work/reservoir, calibration or analysis
boundaries conceal:

- a squared-field response;
- an invalid or target-shaped energy-transfer law;
- circular threshold, calibration or exposure selection?

# Blockers reported against the superseded design

## 1. Unpowered active oscillator

The former parent equation contained a sustaining force and deliberately chose
an injection-lockable limit cycle without identifying a physical PFG-01 pump.
Illumination could therefore modulate energy supplied by the sustaining term and
misattribute it to the optical field. Manufacturer sensitometry did not supply
authority for such a microscopic active oscillator.

**Revision response:** the conclusion-bearing model is now passive, with
positive damping, bounded energy and no sustaining term. Adler is quarantined as
a counterfactual with an explicit pump and no physical-verdict route.

## 2. Rectifying chemical reservoir

The former plan said work was not rectified but then imposed a nonnegative
reservoir driven by signed optical work. Flooring the reservoir could preserve
positive half-cycle increments while discarding later negative work. The dark
subtraction was also inconsistent: dark optical work is identically zero, while
subtracting total dark work would mix thermal or sustaining energy into the
chemical source.

**Revision response:** the reservoir and dark subtraction are removed. Energy
flows through explicit coupled polarization and charge-transfer coordinates;
the donor/acceptor potential pays its own barrier. Dark runs measure fog and
thermal activation.

## 3. Envelope averaging could hide the quadratic law

For a passive linear oscillator, the bilinear work `Q E qdot` legitimately
produces cycle-averaged absorption proportional to field amplitude squared. A
mere ban on an envelope norm cannot distinguish that derivation from secretly
substituting `|A|^2`. The old plan did not freeze the full averaging derivation,
local channel work, intracycle extrema or a full-exposure error certificate
through discontinuous trap thresholds.

**Revision response:** the multiple-scale derivation must precede code and state
reconstructed coordinates, every retained/discarded term, bilinear per-cycle
work, channel fluxes, noise projection and conservative full-exposure event
error. Uncertified threshold cases are unresolved rather than promised an
uncosted direct fallback.

## 4. Non-identifiable one-condition calibration

One uniform exposure could not separately identify optical coupling, transfer,
barriers, site count and development threshold. Even without fitting an
explicit exponent, that freedom could place the final experiment in a
target-preserving linear or saturated regime.

**Revision response:** the one-condition permission is removed. A frozen
multi-condition calibration/holdout design plus pre-prediction Jacobian/profile
identifiability gate is required. Identified composites are stored as
composites; incompatible admissible predictions block interpretation.

# Additional high-priority concerns reported

- Physical noise keys risked including solver/model/arm identity, which would
break paired paths. The revision uses one solver-independent physical bath
identity and derives coarse quadratures from the same fine leaves.
- The former raw/final firewall relied too heavily on forbidden names. The
revision requires import isolation plus semantic dependency mutations.
- Equivalence based on final grain agreement could miss different work and
threshold paths. The revision forbids final-pattern admission and compares
trajectory, channel energy, crossings and slow events.
- Falsification criteria were too qualitative. The revision freezes explicit
dark, wavelength, phase, duration, temperature, dark-delay, sensitizer,
transfer-disabled, zero-net-work and solver controls.

# Important distinction preserved

A derived classical squared-field absorption law is not automatically circular.
For a passive driven coordinate it may follow from the real-time work integral.
The invalid routes are inserting that law before the trajectory, clipping signed
work, borrowing energy from an unaccounted pump, or choosing calibration and
exposure after seeing the comparator.
