# Verdict

**No independently documented powered or self-sustained microscopic oscillatory**
**degree of freedom capable of Adler locking was found in sensitized silver-halide**
**photographic material.** This is a bounded literature finding, not a proof that
such a degree of freedom is impossible.

The documented microscopic picture is instead passive and transient:

1. a cyanine monomer or J-aggregate absorbs the optical field;
2. its excited state relaxes or injects an electron into the AgBr conduction
 band;
3. electrons and holes migrate, recombine, or become trapped;
4. interstitial silver ions and trapped electrons nucleate small silver
 clusters;
5. development amplifies a sufficiently stable latent-image cluster.

That chain can contain resonances, coherent excitons, lattice vibrations and
nonlinear thresholds without containing a free-running oscillator. The current
Born-free plan must therefore not place a self-sustained Adler oscillator on the
PFG-01 authority path unless new material-specific evidence supplies its pump,
free-running phase and injection-locking behavior.

# Decisive test

A candidate counted as Adler-capable only if the evidence supplied all three:

| Requirement | Operational evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Autonomous phase | A free-running oscillation persists without the signal that is supposed to inject-lock it; a frequency, linewidth and phase diffusion can be measured. |
| Maintained energy source | A named pump or negative-damping reservoir replaces the losses and closes the dark energy ledger. Thermal equilibrium fluctuations do not qualify. |
| Injection locking | Detuning and injection strength produce a locking interval/Arnold tongue, phase pulling outside it and phase slips or pull-in dynamics near its boundary. |

This follows the premise of Adler's analysis: an external signal acts on an
oscillator already capable of free-running oscillation. A passive polarization
mode that merely follows a driving field does not become self-sustained because
its forced response has a phase lag.

# What the silver-halide literature actually documents

| Candidate process | What is documented | Why it does not meet the test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cyanine/J-aggregate excitation on AgBr | Femtosecond-to-picosecond fluorescence decay, exciton migration/trapping and electron injection. Components as fast as about 400 fs and average lifetimes of roughly 1–5 ps are reported for tested grains. | The excitation is created by the light and decays. No dark free-running phase or energy-maintaining pump is identified. |
| J-aggregate “coherence size” | Excitons can be delocalized over multiple dye molecules; a reported low-temperature example has an effective coherent size near 18 molecules. | Spatial exciton coherence is not persistent temporal self-oscillation. The same studies measure radiative and nonradiative decay and self-trapping. |
| AgBr carrier and latent-image dynamics | Electron/hole injection, trapping, recombination, lattice relaxation, interstitial-silver neutralization and silver-cluster nucleation/growth. | These are stochastic transport, relaxation and reaction steps. No autonomous optical-frequency or beat-frequency phase variable is reported. |
| Silver-ion motion in AgCl/AgBr | Thermally activated Debye hopping and a conductivity maximum near 500 GHz attributed to large-amplitude ionic vibrations. | A damped thermal relaxation/vibrational spectrum is not a negative-damping limit cycle and no optical injection-locking experiment is reported. |
| PFG-01 plate response | Fine-grained red-sensitive AgX intended for CW recording near 640 nm; the manufacturer specifies a characteristic exposure near 100 microjoule/cm2 and development/bleach chemistry. | The specification is sensitometry, not evidence of a microscopic pump, free-running phase or Adler tongue. |

external signal of similar frequency; it does not turn a passive resonance
into an autonomous oscillator.
- [The photophysics of silver-halide imaging materials](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15012408/)

- [Energetics and dynamics of excited sensitizing dyes on silver-halide](https://library.imaging.org/admin/apis/public/api/ist/website/downloadArticle/jist/41/3/art00014)

describes exciton/phonon coupling, fluorescence decay and destruction of

- [Calculated properties of Ag clusters on silver-halide cubic surface](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jp971625v)
[sites](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jp971625v) describes conduction-band
electron trapping, interstitial-silver neutralization, lattice relaxation and

- [Dynamic aspects of Frenkel-defect formation in AgCl and](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/mrs-online-proceedings-library-archive/article/abs/dynamic-aspects-of-frenkel-defect-formation-in-silver-chloride-and-silver-bromide/F54D24ECA1915B0E36F535732515074E)

# Near misses that must not be promoted into evidence

| Literature phrase or system | Correct interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| “Coherent size” of a J-aggregate | Exciton delocalization over a finite molecular domain, followed by decay; not a free-running clock. |
| Optical polarization or plasmon resonance | A driven passive mode can resonate and exhibit a phase lag while drawing all of its energy from the applied field. |
| Rabi oscillations/strong coupling | Coherent exchange in an engineered cavity or hybrid nanostructure can be transient and externally driven; it does not establish a maintained oscillator in PFG-01. |
| Optical bistability/self-pulsing in a cavity | The cavity, feedback and pump are essential parts of that active system and are absent from an ordinary sensitized plate. |
| Standing-wave phase locking in holography | This is phase coherence of the two optical beams and their Maxwell field, not injection locking of a material oscillator. |
| Oscillatory silver-colloid chemistry in fueled suspensions | Externally fueled colloids under different chemistry and illumination are not AgBr grains in a gelatin holographic emulsion and do not supply optical-phase Adler locking. |

# Search boundary

The audit covered combinations of silver halide/AgBr/AgCl, cyanine sensitizer,
J-aggregate, exciton, self-oscillation, phase locking, injection locking, Adler,
coherent dynamics, ionic vibration and latent-image formation. It considered
primary photographic-material papers, specialist silver-halide proceedings,
manufacturer PFG-01 data and the original Adler-locking premise. No positive
material-specific report was found.

The negative conclusion is limited in two ways:

- absence from the searched literature is not a theorem of nonexistence;
- proprietary emulsion chemistry may be incompletely disclosed, but an
undisclosed mechanism cannot serve as independent authority for a scientific
model.

# Consequence for the technical plan

Do **not** revise the plan by inventing a van-der-Pol/Duffing sustaining term for
PFG-01. The evidence-supported physical authority should begin with a passive,
damped polarization/exciton/charge-transfer system and an explicit thermal bath,
then carry charge trapping, silver-ion motion, cluster nucleation and development
thresholds. Classical squared-field absorption may emerge from the work done by
the real electric field on that passive system; it must not be installed as a
rate law.

An Adler branch may remain only as an **exploratory counterfactual** whose pump
energy is explicit and whose output is prohibited from supporting claims about
real PFG-01 material.

# Evidence that would change the verdict

A future positive result must document, in a sensitized AgX emulsion or a named
microscopic constituent under plate-relevant conditions:

1. oscillation persisting when the optical injection is removed while its
 sustaining pump remains;
2. the pump reservoir and a closed energy balance;
3. a measurable free-running frequency, linewidth and phase diffusion;
4. locking range versus detuning and injected field amplitude;
5. phase pulling/slips outside the range and stable phase offset inside it;
6. survival of those observations across controls that remove the sensitizer,
 AgX grain or pump.

Until then, the status is **insufficient physical authority for a**
**self-sustained Adler–silver-halide model**.
