# Round 1 — independent assessment

## Common prompt

Independently review the frozen Born Selection manuscript. Do not inspect prior review folders or other participant documents. Identify no more than five load-bearing findings. Prefer decisive analysis over comprehensive commentary.

For each finding provide:

1. claim and exact location;
2. charitable reconstruction;
3. classification and severity;
4. concise technical analysis or evidence;
5. plain-English lesson;
6. proposed correction or burden of proof;
7. effect on U1–U7 and the author's clarified central idea;
8. what would change your mind.

End with the strongest surviving contribution, the strongest objection, and at most three questions for the round table. Target 1,200 words or fewer; place necessary derivations in a compact appendix.
