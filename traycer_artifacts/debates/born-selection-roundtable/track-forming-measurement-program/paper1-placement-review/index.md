# Cold review: Paper 1 track-forming placements

## Verdict

The detector-class correction is necessary, but Placements A–D should **not be inserted as written**. The strongest material is the first-mark boundary and the three claims that prevent Mott/conditional-track overreach. The weakest material is the proposed theorem scoping: it treats Paper 1's mathematical share results, its absorptive physical realization, and its multi-quantum extensions as one indivisible package.

Recommended editorial outcome: **revise A, rewrite B, accept C with precision edits, and accept D after narrowing its model claims**.

Reviewed against the [candidate passages](../paper-update-candidates), the current [Paper 1 draft](~/Projects/Physics/DiracKuramotoFramework/drafts/PAPER1_DRAFT_born_selection.md), the [cloud-chamber/SPAD first-mark audit](../../cloud-chamber-spad-first-mark-comparison), and the [three-mark model plan](../three-mark-two-rail-model). No artifact comments were open at review time.

## Priority findings

### High — A and B over-narrow the theorem scope and conflict with §6

Placement A says the “present single-quantum theorems apply directly to the absorptive event class,” while B says the conserved shares in all of §§3–6 refer to one elementary absorptive event. Those formulations erase distinctions present in the manuscript:

- Theorems 1 and 3 are mathematical results about a specified stochastic share process; they are not intrinsically absorptive.
- Theorem 4 is an abstract stopping-time result and could be reused for a track-mark hazard if a track-specific share and rate law were independently derived.
- Theorem 2's unit boundary and Theorem 5's physical vertex-counting argument are tied to the paper's absorptive registration model.
- §6 explicitly includes multi-quantum sectors and nonlinear absorbers, so it cannot truthfully be scoped to “one elementary absorptive event.”

The correct boundary is narrower: **the paper's present physical realization of the single-quantum Born game is absorptive; its abstract martingale lemmas may be reusable at a track vertex only after track-specific stakes, conservation, dropout, commitment, and exclusivity are derived.** Without that sentence-level distinction, the repair unintentionally changes the advertised theorem scope rather than clarifying it.

### High — “retain P4 and add a second axis” is not enough

P4 currently turns absorber spectrum into a universal registration taxonomy. A discrete atomic spectrum does not make every interaction destructively absorb the traveller: a charged projectile can excite or ionize a discrete atom and continue, and scattering channels can coexist with terminal capture. Conversely, continuum matter can participate in a completed absorptive event.

Destructiveness is a property of the **interaction channel/event**, not of the detector material alone. P4 may retain its discrete/continuum distinction only inside the already-declared absorptive model. The track-forming channel must be stated as outside that exhaustive taxonomy, not merely as a second label attached to it.

### High — the proposed track ledger and “mark” vocabulary collapse distinct stages

Placement B's three-item ledger omits energy–momentum carried by material recoil, emitted quanta, and environmental degrees of freedom unless “local deposited excitation” is defined to include them. Placement A then calls every visible track a sequence of coupling–selection–registration events, while D's baseline model contains reversible two-state excitations explicitly described in the plan as **not irreversible macroscopic registers**.

Use three separate terms:

1. **partial interaction vertex** for the branchwise scattering/ionization event;
2. **record-like local excitation or candidate mark** in the unconditional reversible model; and
3. **durable actual mark** only after the extra one-world/commitment condition is stated.

A small deposit can make alternatives highly distinguishable only when the associated conditional material/environment states have sufficiently small overlap. Deposit size alone does not guarantee “nearly complete which-path information.”

### Medium — C is directionally right but attributes too much to “later marks”

Mott's 1929 result supports a sharper claim: the **joint multi-atom ionization amplitude** is concentrated on approximately collinear configurations. Operationally, once an earlier location is specified, the conditional support for later ionizations lies mainly along a compatible ray. It does not produce a single later record history or promote an ionization amplitude to an actual durable mark.

Replace “account for strong correlations among later marks” with “account for strong correlations among candidate ionization records/joint amplitudes,” and name Mott. “Least destructive form” should become **non-absorptive form**: the projectile survives, but the local atom and chamber can still undergo strongly irreversible ionization and nucleation.

### Medium — D asks the minimal model to establish things it cannot test

The three-mark plan predicts a superposition of correlated, branchwise record histories. It does not make one traveller undergo three unique actual deposits, and its two-state local systems are reversible microscopic excitations rather than durable marks. The open problem should say **each branch contains up to three partial transfers** and should require an actuality audit after the unconditional result.

No-signaling is not testable in the minimal co-located two-rail, three-layer model. It belongs to a later spatially separated extension with controllable remote settings. Keeping it in the first-rung acceptance test makes the open problem look more complete than its degrees of freedom permit.

### Medium — the replacement “full quantum to one channel” still needs a calorimetric guardrail

The first claim-to-narrow correctly retires “one site receives the full quantum,” but “routes the full incident quantum to one detector channel” can still imply that all energy resides at one microscopic location. For a completed photon-absorption event, the defensible statement is that the whole photon excitation is removed from the travelling field and its energy is accounted for across the correlated detector/bath products of one registered channel. This is conditional on absorption; reflected, transmitted, scattered, and no-click components require their own ledger.
