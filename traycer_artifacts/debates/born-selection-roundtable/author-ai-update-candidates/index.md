# Born Selection — author–AI update candidates

## Purpose

This ledger records ideas developed through John Bramble's direct collaboration with an AI model. They are **candidates for learning and later editorial judgment**, not automatically accepted physics and not a substitute for the independent Sol–Fable review.

The purpose of retaining both records is comparative:

- the [multi-model final synthesis](../final-synthesis) records recommendations produced by reviewers working from assigned critical and corrective roles;
- this ledger records John's originating intuitions, the refinements produced in dialogue, and the scientific qualifications added before possible manuscript use; and
- only a later manuscript pass should decide which candidates survive, after checking them against the review findings, the paper's scope, and the required evidence.

### JAI-09 disposition under JAI-10

JAI-09 is retained as mechanism-feasibility history, but its claim is narrowed. The semicircle-area calculation may explain a coupling-squared flux under its assumptions; it defines neither a detector-microstate measure nor a material commitment hazard. Until an independently supported oscillator and reduction exist, Arnold-tongue and phase-sweep results are nonphysical fixture tests of synchronization and stopping rules. They are not evidence of individual selection or a derivation of the Born square.

### JAI-11 terminology and research boundary

The originating intuition is retained without treating the computer analogy as established ontology:

- **Normalization** fixes a state's scale—for example, total probability one. It is not primarily a remedy for extreme numerical values.
- **Renormalization** in quantum field theory relates regulator-dependent or “bare” quantities to finite measured parameters. This is likely the source of the remembered Feynman discomfort.
- **Numerical normalization or rescaling** is a computational technique for avoiding overflow, underflow, or loss of precision. It describes our algorithms, not automatically nature.
- **Heisenberg uncertainty** constrains the simultaneous spreads of noncommuting observables, such as position and momentum. It is not presently evidence that nature loses precision while calculating short wavelengths.

The deferred research question is narrower: **Could independently specified ultraviolet, discrete, finite-information, or unresolved environmental dynamics create effective stochasticity that is experimentally distinguishable from standard quantum uncertainty and decoherence?** Any later study must specify the dynamics before fitting quantum statistics and must allow the answer to be no.

### JAI-12 evidential boundary

There is a real physical foothold: gravitational potential differences produce measurable relative phases in coherent matter waves. The Planck-mass crossover also identifies the regime where quantum and gravitational length scales meet. Neither observation supplies evidence for an Adler/Kuramoto locking interaction. A later DK investigation must therefore keep three propositions separate:

1. **Established:** gravity affects wave-packet propagation and interferometric phase.
2. **Open:** a wave packet's own gravitational field causes measurable nonlinear localization or phase coupling.
3. **DK hypothesis:** gravity contributes a specific synchronization interaction that participates in individual record formation.

Only the first proposition may be treated as existing empirical support. The second and third require a covariant equation, a closed energy/conservation ledger, and a discriminating prediction rather than an analogy to the Compton–Schwarzschild crossover.

## JAI-05 detail — particle tracks as the registry's physical motivation

Independent pressure test: [Cloud chamber–SPAD first-mark audit](../cloud-chamber-spad-first-mark-comparison).

### Provenance correction and existing development

This is not a new idea originating in the present exchange. John had already developed it with Claude Opus during earlier framework work. The surviving repository record makes the lineage unusually clear:

- [July 4 three-stage-measurement discussion](~/Projects/Physics/DiracKuramotoFramework/discussions/2026-07-04-three-stage-measurement.md:75) records the detector taxonomy “from the Opus 4.8 thread”: a cloud chamber has one particle, many vertices, with one local commit per vertex.
- The same discussion later implements the click-to-track continuum and Mott conditioning in a toy simulation, explicitly recording what is input and what emerges: [Mott track analysis](~/Projects/Physics/DiracKuramotoFramework/discussions/2026-07-04-three-stage-measurement.md:244).
- [Paper 2's detector taxonomy](~/Projects/Physics/DiracKuramotoFramework/drafts/PAPER2_DRAFT_heisenberg_cut.md:106) already describes a track as a correlated cascade of partial locks whose later stakes are conditioned by earlier registrations.
- [Paper 3's cloud-chamber account](~/Projects/Physics/DiracKuramotoFramework/current_revision_DK_paper.md:1220) already treats the chamber as many capture–commit–register vertices rather than one extended measurement.

The present update is therefore a **cross-paper connection**, not a claim of new origin: Paper 2 and Paper 3 already describe the unitary/conditional evolution that builds a track; Paper 1 can use that established framework element to expose exactly what remains unresolved at the track's beginning.

### Proposed paper use

Use the cloud-chamber observation as the physical motivation for a **local event registry**:

<user_quoted_section>A particle track is not one isolated click but a persistent sequence of mutually consistent material changes. The first committed ionization creates a record that conditions the geometry and available interactions of later ionizations; amplification makes the accumulated registry visible as a track. Then state the limitation immediately:

<user_quoted_section>Mott's wave-mechanical analysis explains why later ionizations are strongly correlated along a track even when the emitted wave is initially spherical. It does not explain why one particular first mark becomes the single actual beginning of the track. In the present framework, the first-mark problem remains the Born-selection problem, while the subsequent track illustrates how a committed registry can persist and constrain later events.</user_quoted_section>

### Role in the three-paper progression

| Paper | Track-registry role |
| --- | --- |
| **Paper 1 — Born Selection** | Ask why this particular first ionization/seed becomes actual and why first-mark frequencies follow the quadratic weights. Use the cloud chamber as a boundary example, not as a solved Born derivation. |
| **Paper 2 — Heisenberg Cut** | Describe each vertex as its own capture–commit–register event and explain how repeated weak records generate a classical-looking trajectory. |

The sticking point is now localized rather than spread across the whole track: **Mott-style conditional evolution explains why a chosen track persists; Born Selection must explain why one first mark begins that actual track.**

- Cite N. F. Mott, “The Wave Mechanics of Alpha-Ray Tracks,” *Proceedings of the Royal Society A* **126** (1929), 79–84, DOI: `10.1098/rspa.1929.0205`.

## Editorial rule for the next manuscript pass

Do not promote an idea because it is intuitively attractive or because several AI models agree with it. For each candidate, require:

1. a plain-English teaching statement;
2. a precise standard/framework/open label;
3. the underlying idea it preserves;
4. the claim it narrows or changes;
5. a non-circular mathematical role;
6. an energy and information ledger; and
7. an observation or calculation that could change the verdict.

John's learning process is itself part of the provenance record, but scientific acceptance remains a separate decision.
