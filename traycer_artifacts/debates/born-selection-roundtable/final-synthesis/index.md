# Born Selection review — final synthesis

## Bottom line

The review does **not** conclude that the central picture—a real Dirac wave packet, continuous one-world evolution, no physical collapse, and spinor-based underlying dynamics—is impossible. It concludes that the current Born Selection manuscript has not yet derived its detector game from that picture.

Its strongest surviving result is a conditional theorem for a single quantum: conserving martingale shares plus a passive linear registration hazard reproduce the initial weights as first-registration probabilities. This is mathematically meaningful and worth retaining. It should not yet be presented as a complete derivation of quantum measurement.

## Claim disposition

| Disposition | Claims |
| --- | --- |
| **Retain** | The martingale/optional-stopping probability lemma; the value of separating continuous physical evolution from an outcome registry; the goal of experimentally distinguishing the mechanism. |
| **Repair and narrow** | Replace the nonconserving independent-noise process with conserving exchange; state the result for a single quantum; distinguish first registration from later energy routing and exclusivity; treat κ_ret primarily as a timescale unless microscopic drift is derived. |
| **Reclassify as consistency** | Multi-quantum Glauber counting, density-matrix affinity, Bell/entangled registry updates, and above-Tsirelson controls until their dynamics is derived independently of Born probabilities. |
| **Derive before claiming** | Ontic site stakes; the conserving detector kernel; irreversible dropout or winner gating; finite non-collapse registry flow; the specific role of Dirac spinors in a detector-dependent channel. |

## Recommended scientific sequence

1. Rewrite the mathematical core as a conditional one-quantum proposition, clearly listing every premise.
2. Carry out the two-site Dirac matter + common field + bath reduction described in the [Round 2 synthesis](../round-02/synthesis).
3. Determine whether the effective process conserves shares, has zero or bounded drift, and supplies either dropout or fast winner routing.
4. Define a finite registry flow and predict the near-simultaneous-commit relaxation window, including the losing wave's post-registration energy and norm.
5. Extend the derived kernel to three sites/two quanta before restoring Glauber, Bell, or above-Tsirelson claims.
6. Only then finalize the in-situ modulation experiment and its ordinary-POVM error budget.

## What the collaboration contributed

- It replaced a shared negative diagnosis—nonconserving noise—with a concrete conserving mathematical model.
- It separated three ideas that the manuscript had combined: fair outcome weights, first detector registration, and complete physical delivery of one quantum.
- It caused both critical reviewers to revise their interpretation of the Dirac-spinor role after checking the companion framework.
- It converted several broad objections into calculations that can actually succeed or fail.
- It reached substantial convergence without asking John to choose a winning model.
