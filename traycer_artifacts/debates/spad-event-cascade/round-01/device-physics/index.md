# SPAD device physics — round 1 record

## Perspective and protected concern

I am the semiconductor/device-physics participant. My protected concern is that the event cascade, its timescales, its energy sources, and its stage boundaries match what real silicon SPADs measurably do — before any framework interpretation is layered on top. Where the framework needs a boundary the device cannot resolve, I will say so rather than let a convenient placement pass as established physics.

Reference device for all numbers below: a thin-junction silicon SPAD, breakdown voltage $V_{\rm br} \approx 25\text{–}30,$V, excess bias $V_{\rm ex} = V - V_{\rm br} \approx 2\text{–}5,$V, depletion/multiplication width $w \approx 1,\mu$m, active diameter $20\text{–}50,\mu$m, junction capacitance $\sim 100,$fF, operated near room temperature with visible-light photons ($\hbar\omega \approx 1.5\text{–}2.5,$eV against silicon's indirect gap $E_g = 1.12,$eV).

## Executive summary (the one-paragraph version for John)

A SPAD click is two very different processes glued together. **Upstream (quantum, femtoseconds to a picosecond):** the photon wave packet is absorbed across the device as a superposition of site-localized electron–hole excitations; one localized carrier pair is what survives — this is where all the quantum weight ($|\beta|^2$) lives, and it is the only stage the framework's selection mechanism needs to explain. **Downstream (classical and thermal, picoseconds to microseconds):** that single carrier drifts into a $\sim 3\times10^5,$V/cm field, starts a branching chain of impact ionization that either dies out (no click, probability $1-P_{\rm trig}$) or runs away to $\sim 10^6$ carriers, crosses a comparator threshold, is quenched, and the diode recharges. Every joule of the macroscopic signal ($\sim$ pJ, about $10^7\times$ the photon's energy) comes from the bias supply; the photon contributes only the $\sim 2,$eV seed and, with it, the *information* of where and when. The decisive device-level fact for this debate: the entire downstream cascade is **photon-agnostic** — a thermally generated carrier (dark count) produces an event-by-event indistinguishable avalanche — so no stage from avalanche initiation onward can be carrying Born selection.

### The energy ledger from the device side

Per click, in round numbers: photon delivers $\sim2,$eV ($3\times10^{-19},$J). The avalanche dissipates $Q \cdot V \sim 10^6,e \times 30,$V $\approx 3\times10^7,$eV $\approx 5,$pJ — a factor $\sim10^7$ larger, all drawn from the bias supply/junction capacitance. Named sinks, no "vacuum": reflected/transmitted optical modes (stage 0), lattice phonon bath (stages 1, 3, 4, 5, 7), secondary emitted photons (stage 5), circuit Joule heat (stages 6–8), trap binding energy temporarily (stage 9). The photon's own $\hbar\omega$ ends up entirely in the phonon bath and carries **no imprint on the output**: Geiger-mode charge is independent of photon energy — the click is binary, and wavelength information survives only statistically (via absorption-depth-dependent jitter), never per event.

## Boundary verdicts

The shared task asks where capture, selection, commitment, amplification, registration, and reset occur. My verdicts, with the two conflations to avoid called out explicitly:

| Boundary | Placement | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Capture** | Stage 1: the absorption vertex, completed (made practically irreversible) by e–h continuum coupling and first phonon emission within $\\lesssim100,$fs | Established **[S]** |
| **Selection** | Stage 2: somewhere between the vertex and sub-ps lattice decoherence. Device physics **cannot resolve** the placement inside that window — everything measurable downstream is classical transport of an already-localized carrier | Bounded, not located **[O]** |
| **Commitment** | Stage 5: the avalanche self-sustaining threshold, where extinction probability → 0. Bias-tunable, concrete, matches the repo derivation note | Established **[S]** |
| **Amplification** | Stages 4–5: impact ionization, 100% bias-powered | Established **[S]** |
| **Registration** | Stage 6: the comparator flip — an *engineering convention* placed well after physical irreversibility, not a physical boundary | Established **[S]** |
| **Reset** | Stages 7–8: quench + recharge. Resets the *detector*, not the field | Established **[S]** |

**Conflation 1 — selection ≠ commitment.** The repo note (`single_photon_derivation.md`) places "the Heisenberg cut" at the avalanche-triggering threshold. As a placement of the *irreversibility/record* boundary this is exactly right and I endorse it. But it must not be read as the locus of *Born selection*, and the device gives two independent proofs:

1. **Factorization.** The click probability decomposes as $P_{\rm click} = |\beta|^2 \cdot (1-R),\eta_{\rm abs},\eta_c,P_{\rm trig}$. The quantum weight enters only through the absorption factor; $\eta_c$ and $P_{\rm trig}$ are classical thinning factors you can tune with bias and temperature without touching the optics. If selection lived in avalanche survival, $P_{\rm trig}$ would have to know about $|\beta|^2$. It does not — it is set by ionization ratios and geometry.
2. **Photon-agnosticism.** A dark carrier traverses stages 3–8 producing an avalanche indistinguishable event-by-event from a photon-seeded one. Whatever physics decides *which site* and *whether absorbed* has finished before the cascade begins; stages 3–8 contain zero quantum-selection content. Any framework mechanism that locates selection in the avalanche must explain why the identical avalanche also fires with no photon present.

**Conflation 2 — commitment ≠ registration.** The comparator threshold is a real, literal amplitude-threshold clip — but on a *classical current*, nanoseconds after commitment, at a level chosen by the circuit designer. It is the correct home for the "threshold" intuition (see below) and the wrong place to look for anything foundational.

**What the reversible window (stage 4) is and is not.** Between seed injection and self-sustainment there is a genuine window where the event can still evaporate — the avalanche extinguishes with probability $q$, the seed pair's energy thermalizes, and no record forms. This is a real two-outcome stochastic gate, and it is the honest reason the framework's "bistable-amplitude / branching-escape" classification in the repo note is the right dissipative class. But the stochasticity in this window is thermal-environmental (phonon scattering, branching statistics), shared identically by dark counts. It gates *click vs. silent absorption* — not *which outcome of the quantum superposition*.

## Verdict on the raised-platform / rogue-wave analogy

The analogy: ordinary wave components sit below a raised platform; a rare local peak crosses it; the energy above the platform is removed into one detector channel; the rest continues or disperses.

**Where the threshold intuition is genuinely right (keep this):**

- **Click/no-click is a first-passage / branching-survival problem** (stage 4): a fluctuating quantity ($n$) either escapes past a threshold or dies. The platform picture maps cleanly onto McIntyre triggering statistics.
- **Registration is literal threshold clipping** (stage 6) — on the classical current.
- **Frequency threshold**: no absorption for $\hbar\omega < E_g$ regardless of intensity — a platform in *photon energy*, the actual photoelectric threshold.

**Where a literal amplitude-platform at the absorption stage fails device facts (this is the load-bearing part):**

1. **There is no intensity threshold for single-photon absorption.** PDE is linear in intensity from saturation down to the dark-count floor; coherent light of arbitrarily low $|\alpha|^2$ produces Poissonian clicks at rate $\propto|\alpha|^2$. An amplitude platform predicts a knee — none is observed. **[S]**
2. **Measured SPAD POVMs are linear.** Detector tomography (Lundeen et al., *Nature Physics* **5**, 27, 2009) reconstructs the SPAD click element as a linear, binomially-thinned function of photon number: $\Pi_{\rm click} \approx \mathbb{1} - (1-\eta)^{\hat n}$ (plus dark term). Any platform model must reproduce an exactly linear POVM — which strips a real-space amplitude threshold of observable content at the single-photon level. **[S]**
3. **No partial clicks, no sub-threshold accumulation.** Heralded single photons give exactly one full-charge click with probability $\eta$, never a fractional event; sub-threshold "remainders" do not accumulate across pulses (no measured memory in PDE at low rates beyond afterpulsing, which has a known trap origin). **[S]**
4. **The beamsplitter anticorrelation kills any *local* platform.** Grangier–Roger–Aspect (1986): one heralded photon on a beamsplitter feeding two SPADs never clicks both (to the measured classical-violation bound). A classical wave with a local amplitude threshold in each detector would sometimes exceed both thresholds. Therefore whatever selects the winning channel must correlate two devices that **share no microstates** — no intra-device nonlinearity, however clever, can do this locally. This is the single hardest device-level constraint on the framework's "detector microstates help select" hypothesis. **[S]**
5. **"Energy above the platform is removed" is backwards for a SPAD.** In a click the *entire* $\hbar\omega$ is absorbed (into $E_g$ + thermalized excess); nothing is skimmed off a peak while a sub-threshold remainder of *that same photon* propagates on. And the macroscopic "removed energy" the analogy gestures at is bias energy, not wave energy.

**Best formal disposition** (my recommendation to the analogy/experiment participant): the platform survives as a **first-passage/branching-survival model for the classical gate (stages 4–6)** with a **quantum-weighted initial condition** — i.e., the platform is real but sits in the *carrier-number/current* variable, not in the optical field amplitude. If the framework wants a platform at stage 2 (selection), it must live in configuration/Hilbert space with the "rare peak" generated by detector/vacuum microstate noise — which is then a stochastic-selection model whose burden is to *derive* $|\beta_i|^2$ as its first-passage measure, nonlocally enough to pass the beamsplitter test. "Threshold clipping of the optical amplitude in real space" should be rejected; "nucleation with quantum-weighted seeding" is the salvageable reading. Classification: **first-passage detection (established, stages 4–6) + open nucleation hypothesis (stage 2), not metaphor-only, not real-space clipping.**

## Risks I want on the record

1. **Risk of smuggling Born in at stage 4.** The branching-escape derivation in the repo note is correct and EM-derived, but its two basins (extinction/survival) are *classical* alternatives. Presenting them as "the" two-outcome quantum reduction invites the criticism that the actual quantum alternative (site $i$ vs site $j$ vs no absorption) was resolved off-stage. The note itself admits this ("Born is still not derived") — keep that admission central.
2. **Risk of over-reading the bias.** Bias influences click probability ($P_{\rm trig}$), gain, timing, and direction — everything *classical*. Its influence on the quantum weights is limited to percent-level electroabsorption. Any framework text implying the field "guides" selection would be contradicted by PDE-factorization data.
3. **Risk of ignoring the no-click-after-absorption channel.** With $P_{\rm trig} \approx 0.5$ typical, a large fraction of genuinely absorbed photons produce *no record*. The framework must say whether "selection" happened for these (a localized pair existed, thermalized, left no trace). If selection = localization, then selection demonstrably occurs without registration — which decouples the selection law from the record and is, I think, actually helpful to the framework, but must be stated.
4. **Risk from avalanche back-action.** Stage 5 emits real secondary photons ($\sim3\times10^{-5}$/carrier) *after* commitment; in arrays these cause crosstalk. If anyone proposes amplification back-action participates in selection, the timescales refuse: emission begins tens of ps after the sub-ps selection window closes.

## What would change my mind

- **Measured nonlinearity of the SPAD POVM** at the single-photon level (a knee in PDE vs. amplitude at fixed photon number, or tomography showing non-binomial thinning) → I would take a real-space amplitude platform seriously.
- **Partial-charge or correlated-fractional events** traceable to a single photon across two detectors → local clipping revived, my stage-2/stage-4 separation wrong.
- **Bias-dependence of upstream interference visibility** (detector back-action on selection): if raising $V_{\rm ex}$ on a SPAD in one interferometer arm measurably shifted fringe visibility beyond electroabsorption accounting → selection and amplification are coupled, and my "photon-agnostic downstream" claim fails.
- **Dark-count statistics violating the same avalanche model that fits photon-seeded events** (different buildup statistics, different $P_{\rm trig}(V_{\rm ex})$ law) → the cascade is not photon-agnostic and stages 3–8 could carry selection content after all.
- **A derivation** (from the foundational participant) showing a microstate-driven nucleation process at stage 2 whose first-passage measure provably equals $|\beta_i|^2$ *and* extends nonlocally to the beamsplitter case → the platform analogy graduates from organizing picture to mechanism, and I would help write its device-parameter version.

### Concessions and refinements I adopted

1. **The stage-1/2 boundary is now a three-event structure** (adopted from the foundational participant's two-commitment taxonomy; they in turn adopted this device version). My original blanket claim "capture practically irreversible within ~100 fs" conflated three distinct events:
  - **(a) Photon-annihilation irreversibility** — escape into the e–h continuum plus the momentum-conserving vertex phonon (indirect Si entangles the bath *at* capture), ~fs–100 fs. My original claim; stands. The energy participant's photon-echo caveat is right in principle but has no purchase here: a 300 K interband continuum has no rephasing handle.
  - **(b) Which-site distinguishability** — builds over the phonon thermalization cascade, 10 fs–1 ps. The vertex phonon carries momentum information, not position; it does not localize.
  - **(c) Dynamical seed unrecoverability** — field-driven e–h separation at ~1 ps, after which geminate recombination is impossible. **This, not (a), is the correct "microscopic seed commitment" boundary.** The selection window is bounded by (a) and (c): femtoseconds to ~1 picosecond.
2. **Vertex wording corrected**: the dipole vertex populates *extended Bloch interband modes* weighted by the optical envelope (µm scale); "site-localized e–h pair at the vertex" is an overclaim. The defensible localized basis is **phonon-dressed free-carrier wave packets of ~10 nm extent** (thermal de Broglie ≈ 8 nm), bath-defined during thermalization. Excitons are irrelevant in Si at 300 K (binding 14.7 meV < kT; instantly field-ionized). The field displaces each coherence cell by ~one cell width (~10 nm drift during localization) — genuine pointer-basis shaping with zero effect on Born weights.
3. **Bias-calibration budget extended**: depletion-width modulation of $\eta_{\rm abs}(V)$ (wider depletion captures more of the absorption-depth profile) can exceed the Franz–Keldysh term and is now flagged beside it. All participants adopted this.
4. **Click-probability partition formalized** (with the energy participant): $P_{\rm out} = 1-A$; $P_{\rm abs,loss} = A_{\rm par} + A_{\rm act}(1-\eta_{\rm col})$ (parasitic absorption is deterministic loss, distinct from stochastic recombination); $P_{\rm abs,ext} = A_{\rm act}\eta_{\rm col}(1-P_{\rm trig})$; $P_{\rm click} = A_{\rm act}\eta_{\rm col}P_{\rm trig}$ — read as position-integrated, since $\eta_{\rm col}$ and $P_{\rm trig}$ correlate through absorption depth (electron- vs hole-initiated $P_{\rm pair}$ differ by $k \approx 0.1$–0.5 in Si).
5. **"Statistically reversible" scoped precisely**: the sub-critical window's reversibility is contingency of the *record* (finite extinction probability $q^n$), not reversal of dissipation — every ionization and phonon emission is ordinary irreversible microdissipation. And "recordless" extinction is accurate only to ~10⁻⁵: extinct chains can fill deep traps and leave a faint afterpulse-statistics trace.

### New quantitative results supplied to the round

- **Committor surface, computed**: extinction of an $n$-carrier avalanche ≈ $q^n$, so $N_*(x,V;\varepsilon) = \ln\varepsilon/\ln q$ with $q = 1-P_{\rm pair}(x,V)$ from Oldham–McIntyre. Numbers at $\varepsilon = 10^{-6}$: $N_* \approx 20$ carriers at $P_{\rm trig}=0.5$, $\approx 130$ at $P_{\rm trig}=0.1$, $\approx 12$ at 0.7. Edge width $\Delta n \approx 1/\ln(1/q)$: 1–2 carriers at moderate overbias. The platform edge's height *and* sharpness are bias-tunable.
- **T8 (avalanche reach-back) closed as a desk check**: $n \lesssim 10^2$ carriers in a ~0.5 µm filament screen ~500 V/cm inside $\varepsilon_r = 11.7$ silicon — ~0.2% of the junction field — and the sub-critical phase is causally *after* the sub-ps capture window anyway. The selection/amplification separation is safe.
- **Ledger small terms**: secondary hot-carrier photons ≈ 30 per event ≈ 60 eV ≈ 2×10⁻⁶ of the ~5 pJ ledger; trap storage tens of eV; recombination-luminescence bound set by Si internal radiative efficiency ~10⁻⁶ (≤10⁻⁴ worst case) — the absorbed-no-click channel is genuinely dark.
- **Double-commit bound instrument** (for the foundational participant's registry law): excess coincidences per herald between two housing-separated SPADs, accidental floor ~5×10⁻⁸ per herald at DCR = 100 Hz and 1 ns window → bounds of order 10⁻⁷ per herald achievable, converting to an extremely tight $O(\Lambda\tau_{\rm drain})$ constraint given the ~1/ps commit hazard scale.
- **T10 (weak-absorber interferometry) re-scoped**: a biased Si element cannot implement the reversible-coupling leg — absorbed amplitude decoheres in ~10–100 fs, so the discriminating term integrates over femtoseconds only. Recommended substitute: a rephasable quantum-memory absorber (Pr:YSO / Eu:YSO AFC or cold atoms, µs–ms coherence) as test arm, with the Si absorber as irreversible-absorber null control.
