# Round 1 — construction and cross-review

## Shared task

Construct a detailed SPAD event cascade from the incoming one-photon wave packet through detector reset. Mark exactly where capture, selection, commitment, amplification, registration, and reset occur. For every stage record:

1. physical degrees of freedom and an illustrative state/equation;
2. what energy moves and where it comes from;
3. whether the step is reversible in principle and in practice;
4. whether stochasticity is fundamental, environmental, effective, or merely ignorance;
5. whether applied electrical bias influences click/no-click, site selection, gain, or direction;
6. standard result versus framework hypothesis versus open calculation; and
7. what observation would verify or falsify the placement.

Pressure-test the author's raised-platform analogy: a distributed wave has ordinary components below a threshold; a rare local peak rises above the platform, the energy above the platform is removed into one detector channel, and the rest continues or disperses into the surrounding field/vacuum. Determine whether this is best modeled as threshold clipping, absorbing boundary, nonlinear focusing/nucleation, first-passage detection, or only metaphor. Do not allow energy to disappear into “the vacuum” without naming the receiving field/bath modes and a conservation law.

Use the repository's `single_photon_derivation.md`, the Born Selection review synthesis, and primary detector/QED sources as appropriate. Do not edit the manuscript.
