# Final synthesis — SPAD selection, avalanche, and the repaired platform picture

## Selected answer

A physically honest SPAD account must separate five events:

1. **Capture:** the photon couples into extended silicon interband modes and is annihilated in each absorbed component.
2. **Selection:** a one-world rule must make one bath-defined carrier channel actual; standard unitary evolution and decoherence do not provide that rule.
3. **Seed and click commitment:** field-separated carriers form a microscopic seed at about 1 ps; only later does avalanche survival become nearly certain at a bias-dependent committor.
4. **Amplification and registration:** the bias supply powers the avalanche, and a comparator writes the digital record.
5. **Quench and reset:** circuitry stops the avalanche, recharges the detector, and manages trap memory.

The cascade is detailed in the [Round 1 synthesis](../round-01/synthesis).

## Decision on the platform/rogue-wave idea

Preserve the threshold intuition but change its physical meaning. Do **not** describe the photon as a classical crest whose portion above a platform is removed while the rest disperses into the vacuum.

Use two distinct thresholds:

- Upstream, an **absorbing boundary in proposed ontic share space**. This represents the missing selection theorem and remains open.
- Downstream, a **carrier branching committor**. This is established SPAD physics and describes whether an already selected seed becomes a click.

The photon contributes eV-scale seed energy. A representative avalanche dissipates pJ-scale energy—about `10^7` photon energies—drawn from the detector's junction field and bias supply. All sinks must be named: phonons, circuit heat, secondary radiation, and traps.

## Unresolved fork

The review intentionally leaves two framework completions open:

- **Passive beable plus unitary wave:** one material configuration is actual while the full wave persists. This best preserves “no physical collapse,” but it owes the Born distribution, exclusivity, empty-wave efficacy, and stress-energy account.
- **Continuous winner-routing:** a new law suppresses or reroutes losing components. This supplies literal winner dynamics but is continuous objective reduction under ordinary terminology and owes explicit energy currents, no-signaling behavior, and a finite double-commit prediction.

Do not blend their energy language. The former has no loser drain; the latter must derive one.

## Next scientific move

The keystone task is a two-site absorber + electromagnetic field + bath reduction that does not use outcome-conditioned quantum jumps. Determine whether it yields ontic conserved shares, fair drift, physical dropout, and one-winner exclusivity. Only then should the framework choose between the two ontologies or attach numerical predictions to the experiments.

Experimental priorities are: twilight factorization, heralded separated-absorber double-seed bounds, reversible-absorber visibility, the four-channel no-click ledger, and dark-count/temperature correlation tests.
