# SPAD event cascade — Sol and Fable collaboration

## Question

What is the most physically accurate, framework-compatible event cascade for a single-photon avalanche diode, and exactly where should **capture, selection, commitment, amplification, registration, and reset** be placed?

The collaboration must also determine whether the author's raised-platform/rogue-wave analogy can be made into a useful threshold model without violating the energy ledger or silently importing collapse/Born selection.

## Author's hypothesis to preserve and test

- The photon and detector-electron states are physically real, delocalized waves.
- Their interaction creates a distributed excitation pattern.
- A small stochastic contribution from detector/vacuum microstates helps select a localized electronic channel.
- Proposed analogy: ordinary waves remain below a raised platform, while a rare peak crosses it and has the energy above the platform removed. The remaining wave continues or dissipates into the surrounding field/vacuum.
- After the microscopic event, the detector's electrical bias supplies directional, strongly amplifying dynamics.
- No physical wave-function collapse or many-world branching is intended.

## Communication rules

- English only; concise, teaching-oriented exchanges.
- Collaboration, not model competition.
- Every stage must state: input state, physical degrees of freedom, governing interaction, output state, reversibility, energy source/sink, stochastic role, bias role, and observable evidence.
- Separate standard detector physics, framework hypothesis, and open derivation.
- No manuscript edits. Participants write only their assigned artifact and exchange messages.
