# Authors' response — dispositions, proposed by the first author for the sponsor's adjudication

*Written 2026-09-05 after reading all four reviews. Nothing below has been applied to the paper: the sponsor asked to read the reviews first, and several dispositions change what the paper claims rather than how it says it. Each finding is given one of four dispositions: ACCEPT (a wording or citation fix for v1.1), RUN (a calculation the record owes before the claim can be restated), CONCEDE (withdraw the claim), REBUT (with the argument). The sponsor's adjudication is required where marked.*

## Findings that change claims (sponsor's adjudication required)

**N1 — the postulate fails for time-separated detectors (R3 M1; fix from R1 M5).** RUN, then ACCEPT. The record's races exposed both channels synchronously and fitted only the conditional ratio; R3's arithmetic is right that a hazard on the whole quantum's survival sends the click to the nearer detector and makes single-detector efficiency exponential in intensity. The fix both reviewers point to is the same: the hazard must act on the *branch* weight of the conditional state, with the no-jump evolution renormalizing the unabsorbed branches — which is the quantum-jump unravelling (Srinivas–Davies; Dalibard–Castin–Mølmer; Carmichael; Wiseman–Milburn) read as what happens. Two consequences the sponsor must weigh. (i) Clause 2 becomes "the projection postulate with Born weights, applied at the vertex", and §5.2's "specification" reduces to "supply the Born rule and nonlocal exclusivity" — R3's reading; the paper should say so. (ii) The no-jump renormalization is itself nonlocal (a null at the near detector raises the far detector's branch), which belongs in clause 3's price. *Owed run:* the two-channel race with channel B's envelope delayed by many commitment times, reporting unconditional click probabilities, to show the failure in the record's own model and close the loop. Then rewrite §5.1 clause 2 and §5.2.

**N2 — the Γ ≫ K regime (R1 M2).** RUN, then ACCEPT or CONCEDE. The 5-second script at Γ/K = 10, 100, 1000 decides whether the crossover goes record-set; the adiabatic-elimination argument says it does. If so: restrict the location claim to Γ ≪ K (rare-earth memories, cavity QED), say the room-temperature semiconductor crossover is record-set and temperature-dependent, reconcile the §4.2 proxy with the §7 condition, and delete "coupling-set rather than temperature-set" from the abstract's list of what is distinctively the paper's (also N9: it is decoherence's own placement).

**§4.5 — in principle, not FAPP (R1 M3, R2 M6, R3 M5; ledger 2).** CONCEDE, subject to the sponsor. Three reviewers independently show the claim is definitional in §4.1, thermodynamic-FAPP in §4.5 (Bennett: Landauer prices erasure, not reversal), supported by citations that presuppose imperfect reversal, and — R3's point — idle for the outcome problem, since what gives one world is the §5 event. The record's own E-14 already rejected "unrecorded" for "re-randomized". The first author's recommendation: withdraw §4.5's claim, state that in-principle irreversibility is entailed by the §5 postulate and by nothing else, delete open problem 2, and cite Bennett. This removes the paper's "one claim that is its own"; what remains distinctive is then the address (narrowed per N7) and the no-go of N16.

**N3 — clause 3 on a three-space field (R2 M1) and N15 — the bifurcated ontology (R3 M3).** ACCEPT the diagnosis; the sponsor decides the ontology. Clause 3 must be restated as a projection in Fock space (the many-photon wavefunction, not the Maxwell field), the §6 caveat extended to all multi-photon states, HOM stated in sink language, and §3.1 rewritten either to adopt configuration-space realism explicitly (Albert; Ney & Albert; Maudlin) or to say that a three-space real-wave ontology for the multi-photon sector does not yet exist (Norsen 2010). PBR and Harrigan–Spekkens to be cited; the fermionic beable (Grassmann-valued classical Dirac field, Struyve 2010) to be named or the "Dirac field" commitment reworded.

**N7 and the TI comparison (R4 M1–M6).** ACCEPT in full. §2 and §5.3 to be rewritten: state what RTI already claims (cut at atomic absorption, coupling as the variable, physical not conventional), then what this paper adds — the rate-balance form, its test, the dense-record requirement, the echo/flip distinction — and no more; concede that Kastner & Cramer claim a derivation of the square and either enter that dispute with Marchildon 2017 and Kastner's reply cited or delete "neither derives the square"; replace Boisvert & Marchildon with Marchildon 2017; quote Kastner's spin caveat and drop "sets spin aside" from the list of what TI lacks; add the foliation to the cost comparison and say TI claims to avoid it; distinguish Cramer's TI from Kastner's possibilist PTI on the reality of the wave; cite Cramer & Mead 2020, Hoyle–Narlikar 1969, Pegg 1975, Kastner 2017 and 2020.

**N6 — the foliation (R3 M4) and N5 — the frame tests (R2 M2, R3 M4b).** ACCEPT. State the foliation as a choice with alternatives (Tumulka 2006; Bedingham 2011; Pearle 2015; Dürr et al. 2014), argue for it or mark it as unargued; rewrite the Bohm contrast to say both foliations are unobservable; withdraw the frame tests from §7 or restore the long form's H′ apparatus and label them tests of extra postulates; cite Zbinden 2001, Stefanov 2002, Salart 2008; qualify the abstract's "no prediction differs" as conditional on a local frame; state ledger E-12's fork; drop "confined to measurement" or define the measurement sector physically and accept the ubiquity (R3 M4c).

R3's suggestion is that the paper's real contribution is the no-go it states in passing: detector dynamics cannot move the Born weights, and with N1 the "hazard at the absorber" family collapses onto the projection postulate. ## Rebuttals

- **R3 M1's stronger conclusion, that no consistent process exists short of the projection postulate:** partly rebutted. The quantum-jump unravelling *is* a consistent stochastic process reproducing the joint statistics of time-separated detectors; the concession is that it is the projection postulate's branch weights with a no-jump renormalization, not a hazard linear in local energy. The paper should adopt it and say what it is.
- **R2 M8 and R4 M7 on the "tautology":** accepted, not rebutted — but the identity is worth one sentence because it is the reason synchronization was dropped: a graded per-site weight gives Born by competing exponentials, and only a sharp threshold does not.
- **R4 M4 on spin:** accepted; the paper's Dirac field is inert in every calculation and the comparison was not earned.
