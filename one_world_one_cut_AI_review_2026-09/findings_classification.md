## Genuinely new findings

- **N1. The postulate as written fails for time-separated detectors (R3 M1).** With commitment in fs–ps and a hazard on the whole quantum's survival, the click goes to the nearer detector with probability ≈ 1 whenever the arms are unequal — every Bell test; single-detector click probability becomes an exponential of intensity rather than linear (17 % nonlinearity at half intensity for η = 0.5). The record tested only synchronous exposure and fitted only the conditional ratio, which is exactly the quantity that hides this. R3's own diagnosis of the fix: the only process that reproduces quantum mechanics is "the branch is absorbed at rate Γ; the world ends up in that branch with probability |ψ|²" — the projection postulate with Born weights; R1 M5 supplies the same fix from the other side: the quantum-jump unravelling with its no-jump renormalization of the conditional state. **The most consequential finding of the round.**
- **N2. The exact model was never swept into the regime real detectors occupy (R1 M2).** Γ/K ∈ {0.05, 0.2, 1}; silicon sits at Γ/K ~ 10⁵–10⁶. There, adiabatic elimination gives a photon decay rate K²Γ/(Δ² + Γ²/4), a Lorentzian of half-width Γ/2: the crossover is record-set and hence temperature-set, inverting "coupling-set, not temperature-set" for the paper's paradigm detector; the general half-width √(Γ²/4 + 2K²) interpolates. The §4.2 proxy K ~ Γ and the §7 condition Γ ≪ K cannot both hold. Five seconds of compute decide it.
- **N3. Clause 3 on a three-space Maxwell field kills heralded signal photons and gets one HOM case wrong (R2 M1).** "The amplitude at every other site vanishes" applied to a field that does not label its quanta predicts zero heralded counts after the idler click and zero coincidences for distinguishable photons; the only surviving reading presupposes Fock-space labelling — the configuration-space objection at every multi-photon state, not only entangled pairs.
- **N4. §4.5's criterion and §4.3's address come apart under pure dephasing, and the model has no alternatives (R1 M4).** A photon in flight with randomly dephased arms meets §4.5's criterion with no vertex; the exact model is a T1 model with one site and one excitation, so it contains no pointer basis and cannot engage einselection; the site basis in §5 is presupposed where decoherence would derive it.
- **N5. The before-before and sidereal experiments exist with nulls, uncited (R2 M2, R3 M4b):** Zbinden et al. 2001, Stefanov et al. 2002, Salart et al. 2008; and the short form drops the long form's H′ "honesty apparatus" ("the experiment tests that postulate, not the core") while keeping the frame tests.
- **N6. A preferred foliation is a choice with alternatives, not a necessity (R3 M4a):** Tumulka 2006 (flash GRW), Bedingham 2011, Pearle 2015 (relativistic CSL), Dürr–Goldstein–Norsen–Struyve–Zanghì 2014 (covariant foliation from ψ), Myrvold 2002, Aharonov–Albert 1984; "confined to measurement" contradicts §1's von Neumann point since the event occurs at every absorption (R3 M4c); ledger E-12's vacuum-frame vs matter-frame fork is unadjudicated (R3 M4d).
- **N7. The address is anticipated by the relativistic transactional interpretation (R4 M2).** Kastner & Cramer 2018 place the cut "at the … level of absorption by individual atoms or molecules" with the coupling as the variable; Kastner 2017 makes absorption "quantitatively defined". What the paper adds is narrower than §5.3 says.
- **N8. Boisvert & Marchildon 2013 is miscited (R4 M3):** it defends TI in contingent-absorber scenarios; the underspecification critique is Marchildon 2017 (arXiv:1712.03060), with Kastner's reply (arXiv:1712.09697). "Sets spin aside" presents a pedagogical simplification as a structural limitation, and the paper's own Dirac field appears in no calculation (R4 M4). TI claims no foliation, the one cost this paper pays that TI does not, omitted from the comparison; PTI's possibilist ontology is not a neighbour on commitment 1 (R4 M5). Cramer & Mead 2020 gives a microscopic two-atom model of transaction formation, falsifying "without microscopic description"; Hoyle–Narlikar and Pegg missing from the lineage (R4 M6).
- **N9. "Coupling-set rather than temperature-set" is decoherence's own placement (R1 M6):** Joos–Zeh's Λ and Zurek's τ_D are coupling-set and temperature-dependent through the environment's state; listed as distinctive in the abstract, conceded in §7.
- **N10. Registration is not photon-agnostic (R2 M4):** SNSPD internal efficiency is a stochastic function of bias, energy, and position (Renema 2014, 2015; Engel 2015); SPAD avalanche probability depends on generation depth; afterpulsing and crosstalk give clicks with no vertex; dark counts need a background hazard the postulate lacks; in a TES thermalization is the registration. Ledger 12 anticipated the topic; the specifics are new.
- **N11. The rare-earth protocol is not executable as described (R2 M5b):** single-ion single-photon K is kHz, Γ(T) is kHz–MHz across 2–10 K, so Γ ≪ K holds nowhere; AFC retrieval bandwidth is set by the comb; collapse in Γt is the definition of T₂ and the non-collapse branch is Mims decay (Böttger 2006). The transmon dial has in substance been done (Katz 2008, Murch 2013, Hatridge 2013) and vertex latencies are inaccessible to any readout (R2 M5a).
- **N12. Citation mischaracterizations (R1 M8, R3 M8, R4 M9):** Brune 1996 (a D² law with a distinguishability axis §4 lacks); Everett 1957 cited for decision-theoretic Born weights (Deutsch 1999; Wallace 2012); Donadi 2021 covers Diósi–Penrose only, CSL "largely removed" overstates (Carlesso 2022); Penrose filed under "new constants"; Nelson without Wallstrom; Minev 2019 mischaracterized twice; "no interpretation pays neither price" false (retrocausal, superdeterminist, QBist accounts); Zurek 2003 dropped; Bloch & Siegert 1940 and Allen & Eberly uncited.
- **N13. Presentation of the record's own history (R1 M10, R3 minor):** "predictions fixed before results" omits that the load-bearing recoverability definition was changed after a calibration run; "tested before being stated" reverses the order (stated in Paper 2, then tested, with two predictions wrong and one misreading corrected).
- **N14. The 10⁻³ exclusivity figure is loose in both directions (R2 M8, R4 M7d):** quantum-dot sources at g²(0) ~ 10⁻⁴ tighten it tenfold; the superluminality factor is 10³–10⁸ over the arms, not "faster than light crosses the detector"; the race-time-to-physical-time mapping is "a choice" in the record.
- **N15. The ontology is bifurcated and the fermionic beable undefined (R3 M3):** three-space fields in §3.1, a non-separable configuration used in §6; the classical Dirac field is Grassmann-valued (Struyve 2010); PBR and Harrigan–Spekkens uncited; Norsen 2010 is the sustained attempt at local beables. Ledger 11 anticipated the topic; the Grassmann point and the "used, not held open" charge are new.
- **N16. A constructive reframing (R3 M9):** the paper's sharpest content is a negative result stated in passing — a real-wave, one-world, no-new-constant programme forced to reproduce quantum mechanics is pushed to the projection postulate at the decoherence crossover, and nothing in the detector (record, amplifier, cascade) can move the outcome weights; with N1 added, a no-go for the "hazard at the absorber" family. A philosophy venue would count that.
