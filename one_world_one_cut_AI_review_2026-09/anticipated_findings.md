# Anticipated-findings ledger — *One World, One Cut* (short form) round

*Written and committed BEFORE any review was requested; the freeze commit precedes the reviews in git history. Each incoming finding will be classified against this list as ANTICIPATED, ANTICIPATED-SHARPENED, or GENUINELY NEW. Written by the first author, who is also the model behind the reviewers, which is a reason to expect the reviews to find these and a reason to hope they find more.*

## Anticipated major findings

1. **An interpretation with no discriminating prediction is not physics.** The paper says so itself (§1, §7). Reviewers will press that the paper's own accounting leaves nothing testable that quantum mechanics does not already predict, and that the one distinctive claim (§4.5) is an argument. *Status: acknowledged throughout; the reply is that checkability of premises about detectors is the offered value, and that the honesty is the point. A reviewer may reasonably find that insufficient for a physics venue.*

2. **§4.5's in-principle claim fails.** Landauer pricing and e^{S/k_B} counting give astronomical, not infinite, cost; Poincaré recurrence is not forbidden; "no agent holds what a reversal requires" is FAPP restated with emphasis. *Status: the paper flags exactly this weakness. Expect reviewers to say the flag does not rescue the claim and to recommend conceding it to decoherence.*

3. **Clause 3 of the postulate is a collapse model without dynamics.** A nonlocal one-quantum constraint acting faster than light, with a preferred foliation to order it, is GRW/CSL's ontological cost without their equations, and the "owed" clearance of Lorentz bounds (§6) is a serious gap. Reviewers may say the paper is Copenhagen-with-realism or "collapse at absorption" and that the combination of ψ-realism with an undynamical collapse is the least stable position in the field. *Status: the price is itemized (§5.1, §6); the covariant formulation is open problem 3. No reply beyond honesty.*

4. **No-signalling "from Gisin's argument in reverse" is not a derivation.** Gisin's theorem says deterministic nonlinear evolution permits signalling; the paper's hazard is stochastic and linear in |ψ|², and marginal invariance under the postulate is a consistency check, not a proof; a reviewer will call the sentence loose. *Status: anticipated; the fix is to state it as a consistency condition explicitly.*

5. **The location result is standard open-systems physics.** A two-level absorber coupled to a photon mode and a dense bath is Wigner–Weisskopf/Fano; the crossover in detuning at the Rabi frequency, the exp(−2Γt f) leak, the Bloch–Siegert shift are textbook. Reviewers will ask what is new besides labelling the detuning "κ_ret" and the half-point "the cut". *Status: the paper concedes decoherence-compatibility; the claimed novelty is the identification of the cut with that crossover and its address. A reviewer may find the identification unmotivated: why is the crossover "the cut" rather than one more coherence scale?*

6. **κ_ret = ΔE/ħ and the identification of detuning with a deficit return rate.** The July panel (N2) already found that detuning is Hamiltonian, not dissipative; the short paper's §4.2 restates the return rate as a deficit rate and the test identifies it with the detuning. Reviewers will say the "location" is a coincidence of two things called κ_ret. *Status: known; the linear model has only the detuning as its return scale, stated in the record; the paper's text may not say this clearly enough.*

7. **The width table is dimensional analysis under an unproven proxy.** K ∼ Γ (passive absorber) turns linewidths into "cut widths"; July finding, repeated. *Status: the proxy is labelled; expect the objection anyway.*

8. **The requirements list (§5.2) rests on unpublished simulations of an idiosyncratic model.** Exponents 1.5, 2.1–2.2, 1.7, 3.2 come from an Adler-oscillator race a reader cannot assess from the paper; "the site count enters as its logarithm" is a claim about a model that has been dropped from the ontology. Reviewers may say §5.2 imports the discarded synchronization program through the back door, or that a specification derived from a wrong model constrains nothing. *Status: anticipated; the reply is that the clauses are generic (memoryless, energy-linear, nonlocal) and that the numbers are cited to the record. A reviewer may still find the section out of place.*

9. **Energy accounting in the sink picture.** "The whole quantum flows through one site" while "the amplitude at every other site vanishes" — reviewers will ask where the energy at the other sites went, and whether amplitude bookkeeping vs energy is consistent with the earlier statement that every site "draws energy in proportion to the square". *Status: real tension in the wording: sites draw energy reversibly (capture) and the drawn energy is returned when the sink opens elsewhere; the paper should say so.*

10. **The transactional characterization.** Kastner's community may object that (a) PTI does define absorbers physically (Kastner & Cramer 2018 is precisely that), (b) "sets spin aside" is a simplification in one paper, not a feature of PTI, (c) TI proponents claim the Born rule is *derived*, so "neither derives the square" is contested rather than settled; and that Cramer's absorber-theory lineage includes Hoyle–Narlikar, Pegg, and Davies more fully. *Status: anticipated; the paragraph follows the sources read, but a specialist could find it uncharitable.*

11. **ψ-ontology asserted, not argued.** PBR (Pusey–Barrett–Rudolph 2012) is not cited; the configuration-space objection is "held open" but a real-wave ontology on R^{3N} is not a real wave. Reviewers will say §3.1 and §6's "restates the real-wave commitment" hide the paper's largest metaphysical cost. *Status: anticipated; PBR should be cited and the objection stated as a cost, not held.*

12. **"Commitment is the vertex" needs detector evidence.** The claim that amplification carries no which-site weight and that the interband vertex plus thermalization (10 fs–1 ps) is the first irreversibility is asserted from timescale arguments; afterpulsing, crosstalk, and trapping in real SPADs are which-site-dependent. *Status: partially anticipated; a detector experimentalist will press.*

13. **The interpretation is indistinguishable from "Copenhagen with a physical cut".** Reviewers may ask what one world plus a postulated stochastic event at a located cut adds to Bohr beyond realism about the wave, and whether that realism is doing any work. *Status: anticipated; the paper's answer is the address and the width, which a reviewer may find thin.*

14. **AI authorship.** The byline lists an AI model as first author; most journals prohibit this. Under this repository's protocol the reviewer is asked to recommend the honest byline; expect at least one reviewer to note that no mainstream venue would accept it as written. *Status: known; the sponsor's decision before circulation.*

15. **Self-containment.** The paper cites a GitHub record for its numbers; a referee cannot verify them from the manuscript. *Status: anticipated; the long form exists for that purpose but is itself unpublished.*

## Anticipated minor findings

- Idiosyncratic terminology ("sink", "hazard", "tongue", "share") without definitions for a general reader.
- The temperature predictions and the coupling dial are described as non-discriminating by the paper itself; reviewers may ask why §7 exists.
- The two-classical-limits and register material (§3.3–3.5) is interpretive decoration with no consequence downstream.
- Table in §4.2 mixes results from different models (linear absorber; Rabi model; classical oscillator) without saying so in the table.
- Reference-list issues: Fermi 1950 as a book for "Golden Rule No. 2"; Steck as a web resource; Schlosshauer & Camilleri as an arXiv preprint.
- Length of §2 relative to the paper's own content.

## What would count as genuinely new

Anything not reducible to the items above — in particular: an error in the exact-model results or their interpretation that the record does not already flag; a demonstration that the graded-eligibility postulate (§5.1 clause 1) plus clause 2 gives a non-Born result in some regime; a named prior work that already states the cut as the vertex crossover with a width; a specific experiment in the existing literature that contradicts a claim here; an internal contradiction between sections that the authors did not see.
