**N2. "γ = ΔE/ħ treats detuning as decay"** (GPT 1; questions 1–3). **Partial accept, with a clarification the reviewer's framing genuinely missed — and a concession of everything that survives the clarification.**
- *The clarification (sponsor-articulated).* The rate γ = ΔE/ħ is not, and was never intended as, dissipation into a bath. A sub-threshold site has no eigenstate at its partial energy (P4(i)); its excitation is virtual, and ΔE/ħ is the rate at which the borrowed energy *reversibly returns to the common field* — the beat rate of a failed lock, the same physics as §5's slip time. The returned energy does not leave the system; it re-enters the exchange pool that the selection game redistributes. The initial interaction of the quantum with the detector bulk is thereby *partially reversible* — categorically different from the later irreversible commit at registration — and this two-process structure is load-bearing for the whole framework (it is why the game can be a martingale at all). The reviewer's bath-decay reading, and its "a detuned state can be long-lived" counterexample, address a different physical situation: a stationary detuned eigenstate, which P4(i) says the sub-threshold site does not possess.
- *The concession.* The text earned the misreading: it says "damped," "decay rate," and uses γ — the universal bath symbol — while carrying an independent Δ in the same equation. Answers to the reviewer's questions 1–3, which v0.3 will build into the text: e < E₀ denotes the site's share of field energy during exchange, an ontic quantity under P0, not an eigenstate occupation; the "bath" receiving the returned energy is the common field itself, reversibly; Δ is the drive–transition frequency mismatch, γ the deficit-induced return rate — two independent parameters describing two different mismatches. And the demand stands in full: nothing yet *derives* the return rate ΔE/ħ, or the legitimacy of modeling reversible give-back as a contraction eigenvalue in the site's reduced description, from a microscopic field + absorber + bath model. **Escalated to Paper 1's open-problems ledger** (it strikes Theorem 2's premise there, not only this paper), with GPT's H = H_field + H_absorber + H_bath + H_int program quoted as the required calculation. Until then, v0.3 labels γ = ΔE/ħ a *physically motivated ansatz* — the reviewer's word, accepted.
- *Rename.* To prevent the same misreading permanently: v0.3 renames the quantity the *return rate* κ_ret = ΔE/ħ, reserving γ and Γ for genuine dissipative rates.

Under that form the panel's own findings are revisable in the ordinary sense: every accepted item above is a bounded textual, figure, or scoping correction, plus honest demotion of the two items (γ-ansatz, K~Γ proxy) that were stated above their support. The distinction between "the strong claim needs new theory" (agreed, and pre-logged) and "the conditional paper needs major revision" (agreed, and undertaken) is exactly the distinction the manuscript's §1 partition was built to hold. We also note that the same reviewer's rubric scores the *conditional* artifact (reproducibility 3, citation 3, novelty 3) at levels consistent with major revision rather than rejection of the enterprise.
