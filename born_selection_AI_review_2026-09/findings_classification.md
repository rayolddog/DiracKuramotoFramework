- **N2. The 470 nm line is a fill-factor artefact and Grangier 1986 used photomultipliers** (R2 M2, M3; R4 M3, M4). A.2's "does not average out with N" is an infinite-mean artefact; the clause is wrong as stated** (R1 M4(a), (b)). *Fix: replace with the light-tail condition; withdraw "the one test the model passes for a reason it supplies itself" (MathX §12.5); say the observable has no power at N ~ 10¹⁰.*
